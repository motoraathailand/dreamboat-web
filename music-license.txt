DREAM BOAT — music license note
================================
All music and sound in DREAM BOAT (soft pads, bell/pluck arpeggios, water ambience,
oar splashes and droplets) is generated procedurally at runtime by this project's own
code (js/audio.js) using the Web Audio API. No audio files, samples, loops, or
third-party/famous melodies are used. Chord progressions and note patterns are original
and partly randomised on every play.

Each of the 8 places has its own original mood; neighbouring moods crossfade smoothly
as the boat travels from one place to the next.

1 "Sunset River"        D major, slow warm sawtooth/sine pads, pentatonic plucks.
2 "Golden Reeds"        G mixolydian, airy open-fifth sine pads, slow breathy plucks.
3 "Old Wooden Bridges"  F major, triangle pads, light kalimba-like plucks.
4 "Misty Valley"        A dorian, very slow soft sine pads, sparse tones.
5 "Firefly Forest"      E major 7 shimmer, tiny high bell twinkles.
6 "Lantern Bridge"      E-flat lydian, triangle pads, music-box style bells.
7 "Starlight Lake"      D-flat add9, wide spacious pads, slow glassy bells.
8 "Dawn Cloud Sea"      C major, gently rising progression, brighter pad.

Created by the project for MOTOR AA. Free to use with the game.
